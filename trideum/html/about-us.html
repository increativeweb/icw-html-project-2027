ABOUT US

We Embrace The World’s Toughest Challenges With a Servant’s Heart

Trideum is a 100% employee-owned company, committed to distinctive quality and unparalleled customer service in all aspects of our business. Trideum provides state-of-the-art innovations, integrations, solutions and support across all of our service areas.

Our employees engage in for-profit work predominantly for the military services and agencies of the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD).

We are headquartered in Huntsville, Alabama, with satellite offices in Leavenworth, KS and Killeen, TX. Our employees operate on 40+ contracts in 25 locations in the U.S. and Kuwait.
