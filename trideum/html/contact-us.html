      • EXPERTISE
        • Design & Engineering
          Our approach includes agile software development and DevOps. We employ Model Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) to provide increased efficiency in the overall approach to the engineering and development lifecycle.
        • Software Development
          Trideum implements a robust Software Engineering process to guide the implementation of software products and services to meet a variety of intended uses.
        • Test & Evaluation
          Trideum provides services in support of all developmental and operational testing phases as well as support to independent evaluations through analysis, experiments, and other types of assessments.
        • Training Development and Execution
          Trideum provides individual training and education as well as collective training and exercises at the individual, small team, collective and large-scale levels.
        • Autonomous Systems
          Trideum specializes in the development of unmanned systems. We work with our clients to understand requirements, design, test, analyze, and implement capabilities for Command, Control, Communication, and Computers (C4) interfaces.
