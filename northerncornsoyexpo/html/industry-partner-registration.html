2027 Exhibit Information

Date:Tuesday, February 9, 2027
Time:7:30 A.M. - 4:30 P.M.
Location:Red River Valley Fairgrounds Butler Arena • West Fargo, ND

*Any weather updates that may be needed will appear on this page.*

Download Expo information HERE or register online to the right. Use this form to hold exhibit space at the Northern Corn and Soybean Expo. Your exhibit space will be reserved upon payment and our receipt of your Expo Contract with a required authorized signature. Space is allocated based on the date of payment receipt.

Exhibit space includes:

  • 10′ x 8′ booth
  • Electricty and WiFi
  • Listing in program book and on website
  • Draped 8′ high back wall and two draped 3′ high side dividers
  • One 8′ draped table and two standard chairs (per booth)
  • Four individual exhibitor registrations (to be completed at a later date) for first booth and two registrations for an additional booth space (maximum of six total exhibitor badges per booth)
