Legally qualified specialists and advisers

Immigration Chambers is a collective of member lawyers and advisers with vast experience in New Zealand immigration law. By law, any person that gives you immigration advice about New Zealand must be licensed by the Immigration Advisers Authority (IAA) unless they are exempt. All our advisers are licensed under the Immigration Advisers Licensing Act 2007, and the lawyers and team at our registered law firm, Fisher Foley Barristers and Solicitors, are all exempt from licensing requirements.
