Types of New Zealand Visas for Individuals and Families

If you’re from another country and wish to live in New Zealand, there are many different visa options that you could be eligible for. Some of the most common visas available for individuals and families include:

Study Visas

If you want to take advantage of the world-class educational facilities available in New Zealand, there are visa options that allow you to study for periods of 3 months to 5 years.
Learn More

Work Visas

Whether you want to have a working holiday, progress your career, or become a resident through your occupation, there is a New Zealand work visa to suit your circumstances.
Learn More

Skilled Migrant Category

If you have relevant skills and qualifications that help to contribute to New Zealand’s economic growth, the Skilled Migrant Category visa could be right for you.
Learn More

Green List Residency Pathway Visa

If you work in an in-demand occupation listed on the Green list, you could be eligible for one of two residency pathways for New Zealand.
Learn More

Partnership-Based Visas

If your partner is a New Zealand citizen, holds a New Zealand resident visa, or holds a New Zealand work or study visa, you could be eligible for a New Zealand visa.
Learn More

New Zealand Permanent Resident Visas

If you hold a New Zealand resident visa, you could be eligible to become a permanent resident of New Zealand and be able to stay indefinitely.
Learn More

Entrepreneur Visa

If you’re an experienced entrepreneur, you could build your own business in New Zealand or buy an existing New Zealand business.
Learn More

Investor Visa

If you’re an experienced investor looking to live, work and invest in New Zealand, you may be eligible for an investor visa, where if you meet the requirements, you could stay indefinitely.
Learn More

Parent Visa

Explore all New Zealand Parent Visa options, from short-term visits to permanent residency, designed to help you reunite and live together.
